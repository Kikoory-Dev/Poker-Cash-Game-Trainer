# Hardin Opening Ranges — Source of Truth

Transcribed directly from *Master Micro Stakes Poker* by Alton Hardin (2017),
Chapter 16 "Raising First In", pages 162-168. This is the AUTHORITATIVE source
for all RFI (raise-first-in) opening ranges used by the tracker flags and the
preflop trainer. The full book PDF is stored at
`reference/Master_Micro_Stakes_Poker_Alton_Hardin.pdf`.

## Shorthand (as written in the book)

| Position | % | Conservative | Moderate |
|----------|---|--------------|----------|
| UTG | 9.80–12.07 | 66+, ATs+, KJs+, JTs, AJo+, KQo | 55+, ATs+, KTs+, QTs+, JTs, T9s, 98s, 87s, AJo+, KQo |
| MP (HJ) | 12.67–15.54 | 55+, A9s+, KTs+, QTs+, JTs, T9s, ATo+, KQo | 44+, A9s+, A5s-A4s, KTs+, QTs+, J9s+, T8s+, 97s+, 87s, 76s, ATo+, KQo |
| CO | 22.17–24.89 | 22+, A2s+, K9s+, QTs+, J9s+, T8s+, 97s+, 86s+, 76s, A9o+, KTo+, QJo | 22+, A2s+, K8s+, QTs+, J9s+, T8s+, 97s+, 86s+, 75s+, 65s, A9o+, KTo+, QTo+, JTo |
| BTN | 35.44–43.29 | 22+, A2s+, K7s+, Q8s+, J8s+, T8s+, 97s+, 86s+, 75s+, 65s, 54s, A2o+, K9o+, QTo+, JTo, T9o, 98o | 22+, A2s+, K5s+, Q6s+, J7s+, T7s+, 97s+, 86s+, 75s+, 64s+, 54s, 43s, A2o+, K7o+, Q9o+, J9o+, T8o+, 98o, 87o |
| SB | 22.17–24.89 | (identical to CO) 22+, A2s+, K9s+, QTs+, J9s+, T8s+, 97s+, 86s+, 76s, A9o+, KTo+, QJo | 22+, A2s+, K8s+, QTs+, J9s+, T8s+, 97s+, 86s+, 75s+, 65s, A9o+, KTo+, QTo+, JTo |

## Key notes
- **T8s IS in the BTN conservative range** (and CO/MP-moderate/SB). Confirmed from book page 166-167.
- SB opens an identical range to CO (book states this explicitly — both 22.17–24.89%).
- The book recommends starting with CONSERVATIVE and widening to MODERATE as you improve.
- The tracker's `HARDIN_RANGES.all` = conservative ∪ moderate. VPIP_TRASH fires when a played hand is outside `all`.
