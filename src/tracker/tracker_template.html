GGPoker color labels (tap to expand)

Framework for labelling opponents via GG color tags. Currently using the 4 ACTIVE colors; the rest are reserve for when you scale up. Mnemonic: warm = wary, green = money/go, p-colors = pending/premium. Labels are hypotheses — keep using Pink until you have a real sample, and re-color when players surprise you.

Red — Dangerous regACTIVE

Strong thinking TAG/LAG. Play straight, do not bluff, do not pay off.

Green — Fish (station)ACTIVE

Loose-passive. Value bet relentlessly, never bluff. Your bread and butter.

Mint — NitACTIVE

Tight-passive, premiums only. Steal relentlessly, fold to aggression.

Pink — Needs more dataACTIVE

Insufficient hands. Tentative, re-evaluate as reads build.

Blood orange — Aggressive maniac

Over-bluffs, applies pressure. Call down lighter; danger is aggression not skill.

Orange — Flawed reg

Solid-ish, makes mistakes. Caution but beatable.

Light green — Trickier fish

Calls too much but occasionally aggressive. Still profitable, slight caution.

Yellow — Calling station (extreme)

Never folds. Value bet huge, never ever bluff.

Blue — Cool reg (unknown)

Competent, controlled, not yet proven dangerous. Default reg bucket.

Purple — Whale / premium target

Huge target. Table-select toward them; sit and stay.

Core 4 covers ~90% of decisions. Reads accumulate mainly in Regular (in R&C you rarely see the same player twice).
